# Logo placement guide

| Placement | Asset | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Dark website header or footer | `02-horizontal-cream-on-green.png` | Full name in the current dark site shell |
| Browser favicon or Apple touch icon | `06-symbol-cream-on-green.png` | Symbol remains recognisable in a square crop |
| Light wide header, document, business card or presentation | `01-horizontal-green-on-white.png` | Horizontal full-name lockup on white |
| Square tile that must show the company name | `04-square-stacked-cream-on-green.png` | Preferred square full-name variation |
| Small light avatar or compact marker | `05-symbol-green-on-white.png` | Symbol-only version on a light ground |
| Narrow name-led space with the symbol identified nearby | `07-wordmark-green-on-white.png` | Wordmark-only version |
| Single-colour printing | `08-horizontal-black-on-white.png` | Monochrome horizontal lockup |
| Square light name tile | `03-square-stacked-green-on-white.png` | Review only pending cleanup |

Keep the supplied proportions and leave clear space around each lockup. The images have opaque backgrounds. Do not stretch, rotate, mirror, recolour, crop, or place them on a noisy background. Do not use a full-name lockup at favicon size. Large-format fabrication and colour-controlled printing need a vector master and supplier proof.

The references in `references/` preserve existing letterhead SVG artwork and its services descriptor; they are not the vector master for the new logo variations.
