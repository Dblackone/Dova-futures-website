Enquiry privacy notice

Updated 8 September 2026.

Dova Futures Limited uses your name, email address, optional phone number, project type and message to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project. Please do not include identity documents, payment details or other sensitive information.

How the form works

Cloudflare hosts this website and processes form requests. An operational copy of your enquiry is kept in a private Cloudflare database for up to seven days to support delivery and recovery. Resend processes the notification email sent to our team. Email providers and the team's mailbox retain messages separately from this short-lived website copy.

Short-lived, hashed network identifiers limit automated abuse. Rate-limit records expire after a 15-minute window and are removed by scheduled maintenance. Hosting and email providers may process technical security and delivery logs under their own policies.

Your choices

Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing. Selecting “Send via WhatsApp” opens WhatsApp with the details you chose to share; WhatsApp then processes that message under its own terms. You may also contact us without using this form.

Contact

For questions about your information, corrections or deletion requests, contact info@dovafutures.com or +234 816 367 5439. The team may need to verify your request before acting on it.
